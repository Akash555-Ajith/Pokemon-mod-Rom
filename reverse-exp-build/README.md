# Pokemon Emerald — Reverse EXP

A separate ROM based on original English Emerald. Starters remain level 5; enemy trainers, their teams and levels, wild encounters, shops, maps and battle-style options remain original. None of the earlier mods are included.

- Defeating an enemy Pokemon subtracts the EXP your eligible Pokemon would normally receive instead of adding it. Participation, Exp. Share, trainer-battle bonuses, traded-Pokemon bonuses and Lucky Egg use the original calculation.
- The loss happens when ordinary Emerald awards EXP, after each enemy faints, including wild battles. Running away without defeating anything does not remove EXP. Battle facilities and other modes which normally award no EXP still award none.
- Levels decrease when EXP crosses a level threshold. EXP never drops below the amount for level 1. Level-100 Pokemon can also lose EXP.
- At the end of battle, evolved Pokemon revert below their original level-evolution thresholds. For stone, trade, friendship and beauty evolutions, each lost level can reverse one stage. Multiple levels lost can reverse several stages. No de-evolution occurs without a level loss, and Pokemon without a pre-evolution stay the same.
- De-evolution is applied directly after battle, without an evolution animation. Moves, held items, custom nicknames and remaining EXP are retained. Reduced maximum HP is handled safely without accidentally fainting a living Pokemon or reviving a fainted one.
- Rare Candies, daycare gains and ordinary forward evolution outside this reversed battle-EXP flow retain their original behavior. EV gains, money and story rewards remain normal.

Open this ROM under its own filename and start a new game. Keep a separate save from your other Emerald ROMs.

Build and validation results will be added after verification. This mod deliberately makes ordinary battles reduce your team's strength; no enemy scaling or other balancing changes were added.
