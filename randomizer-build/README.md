# Four separate Emerald randomizers

Each ROM starts from original English Emerald. Exactly one mod is enabled in each. None of the previous legendary-trainer, fixed-level-100, or seven-rule Nuzlocke modifications are included. Normal shops, encounters, fainting, level limits and battle-style options remain intact.

1. **Random trainer Pokemon and levels:** Each NPC trainer's occupied party slots receive random species from all 386 original Generation I–III Pokemon and independently random levels from 1–100 when battle starts. Team sizes and held items stay the same; moves suit the new species and level. Wild Pokemon and your own party are unchanged. Rematches reroll.
2. **Party shuffle after trainer battles:** Your occupied non-Egg party slots change to different random species after each NPC trainer battle, after the field scripts finish restoring your real party. Wild battles do not trigger this. Levels, EXP progress, held items, nicknames, status and fainted state remain; new species get appropriate moves while existing HM moves are preserved. PC Pokemon and Eggs are unchanged. This applies after wins and losses, with normal whiteout healing on a loss.
3. **Random evolution every level:** Every level gained in battle queues a compulsory random evolution after battle. Multiple levels produce multiple evolutions. Rare Candies also trigger random evolution, and daycare withdrawal applies one random species change for each level gained there. New forms preserve levels, EXP progress, moves, nicknames and items. Fully evolved Pokemon and legendaries can evolve into any other valid species. Everstones and the B button do not stop this rule. Loss/escape applies any queued changes without an animation. Ordinary item and trade evolutions otherwise remain available.
4. **Map entrance randomizer:** Ordinary doors, stairs and cave entrance warps choose destinations from valid exploration warp tiles. A source entrance keeps its destination throughout the run and across saves, using the saved trainer ID as its seed. A new game usually gives a different layout. The introduction stays normal until you receive your starter. Script-only, link, underwater and special facility maps are excluded; walking over route boundaries, Fly, Dive and scripted travel retain their normal behavior. Entrances are not necessarily paired, and this is an exploration shuffle, not a progression-solvable seed generator. While freely moving in the overworld, hold **L + R and press Select** to return outside in Littleroot if you are stuck. Story flags and badge requirements remain normal.

All randomizers are for single-player gameplay; link battles and recorded replays retain their original behavior. Start a new game for each ROM and use a separate save filename. Build and targeted emulator results will be recorded after validation. Full story completion is not guaranteed for the entrance shuffle.
